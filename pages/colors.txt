### My three picks for your game

 I'd personally test these first:

 **🥇 Neon Playground** — safest and most flexible.\
 **🥈 Black & Candy** — strongest visual identity.\
 **🥉 Tropical Arcade** — most unusual and memorable.

 And there's a useful trick with your UI: **don't make every screen use every color**. For example, keep the background/surface colors consistent, then assign one dominant accent per screen:

```
MAIN MENU       → Lime + Cyan
LEVEL SELECT    → Cyan + Orange
GAMEPLAY        → Cyan
YOU DIED        → Red + Orange
SCORE           → Lime + Yellow
SETTINGS        → Orange
```

 That gives the whole game a coherent palette while letting each screen have its own personality.
